Team & Roles

Broker of Record 1

Account owner. Full compliance authority plus billing, workspace settings, and user management.

Alternate BOR 1

The compliance twin. Reviews, clears and attests exactly like the BOR so nothing stalls, without account ownership.

Compliance Officer 1

Reviews and clears deals, works the queue, sends files back. No billing or user management.

Agent 4

Submits their own deals, sees only their own files and reports, and fixes what gets flagged.

Admin capability

Submit on an agent's behalf

Broker of Record, Alternate BOR and Compliance Officer can upload paperwork straight into an agent's account. The deal still lives in the agent's file and counts toward their record, the office just did the submitting. Agents can only submit for themselves.
