How it works

Three steps. That is the whole thing.

No training day for your agents. If they can submit a file, it gets checked before it ever reaches your deal desk.

1

Agent submits the deal

Your agent uploads the agreement and supporting forms, or it flows in straight from your existing workflow.

2

BrokerPass checks every page

Line by line against current RECO, TRESA and OREA rules, plus every date, initial and signature on every page.

3

Your desk gets the pass or the fix list

Green means file it. Amber shows exactly what to fix, where, and who to chase, with one click to send it back.

The deal desk

One board for every deal in the office.

Your broker of record and admin team see every submission, its status, and what is still outstanding. Fix a flag, re-check, watch it turn green.
