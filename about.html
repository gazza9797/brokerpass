Who built this

We have sat at the
deal desk. That is why this exists.

BrokerPass was built by two people who have spent years inside Canadian real estate, not by a software company guessing at the problem.

Every brokerage runs on the same quiet stress. A deal comes in, someone has to check it, and the thing that trips it up is never the big stuff. It is a blank initial on page 3. A missing time on the irrevocable. A signature that never made it onto the amendment. Small misses that turn into a bounced file, a frustrated agent, and in the worst case a call from RECO.

We got tired of watching good deals get held up by things a checklist should have caught.

Between us we have trained thousands of Realtors, rolled out technology across brokerages and boards nationally, and watched the compliance bottleneck from every seat: the agent submitting, the admin reviewing, the Broker of Record signing off. The problem was never that people did not care. It was that catching every date, initial and signature by eye, on every page, on every deal, is not a job humans are good at.

So we built the tool we wished the desk had. Upload the package, get a clear report, fix what is flagged, submit clean. It does not replace the Broker of Record. It just makes sure nothing lands on their desk that should have been caught first.

Our opinion: compliance software has spent years trying to replace human judgement. That is the wrong goal. The Broker of Record should keep the judgement. The software should just handle the tedious first pass so they can use it.

The two of us.

No big team, no outside agenda. Just people who know this industry from the inside.
