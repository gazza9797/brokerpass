The big three
Is BrokerPass giving legal advice?
No. BrokerPass is a compliance aid, a second set of eyes on the file before it reaches your desk. It flags what looks wrong and explains why in plain English. Your Broker of Record still makes every call. It speeds up the review, it does not replace the person doing it.
Does it replace our compliance department or Broker of Record?
Not even close, and that is the point. It does the tedious first pass so your compliance team spends their time on judgement calls, not chasing a missing initial on page 3. Think of it as the tool your admin runs before a file lands in the review queue.
How secure are our files?
Every file is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in a Canadian region, and auto-deleted on a short timer once the check is done. Access is role-based, so only your desk and the submitting agent ever see a document. We are built to SOC 2 controls and issue deletion certificates on request.
What it checks
What exactly does it check against?
The current RECO, TRESA and OREA rules that apply to the deal type, plus the mechanical stuff that quietly kills files: missing dates, blank initials, unsigned pages, and incomplete required fields. Every finding is tagged so you know which rule it maps to.
Which documents can I run?
The common deal packages: agreements of purchase and sale, buyer representation, listing agreements, and amendments, across the standard deal types. PDFs and scans both work. You run one deal package at a time and get one clean report.
Do you handle FINTRAC and AML?
No, and on purpose. FINTRAC and anti-money-laundering is its own lane with dedicated tools. BrokerPass stays focused on getting the deal package clean and ready to submit. Fewer things done properly beats everything done halfway.
What if it flags something that is actually fine?
Every finding carries a confidence level and a plain-English reason, and your desk can dismiss it with a note. Some checks are exact matches, some are judgement flags for a human to confirm. A deal only goes green when nothing critical is open and every confirm item is resolved. You are always the final word.
Fit and rollout
Does it touch our transaction workflow?
No. BrokerPass sits in front of your existing system. Your agents keep working the way they already do, the deal just gets checked before it enters your normal submission flow. Nothing to rip out, nothing to relearn.
Can individual agents buy it?
No. BrokerPass is a brokerage tool. It is built around the deal desk, the Broker of Record and the admin team, because that is where compliance actually lives. Agents submit into it, the brokerage owns it.
How do we get started?
Run a pilot on your last 20 deals. We show you exactly what BrokerPass would have caught before any of them went out. No workflow change, no commitment, just proof. If it earns its place, you turn it on for the office.
